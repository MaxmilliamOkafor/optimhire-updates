Remembered answers

When an application is confirmed as submitted, the answers you (or OptimHire) gave to questions the built-in rules could not answer — or answered differently — are remembered and reused next time the same question appears. Contact details, links, salary, dates and sponsorship / work-authorisation questions are never remembered (those come from your Knockout answers). Forget anything you don't want reused.

Knockout answers

These questions screen applications out automatically: a wrong answer gets you rejected or ranked last, whoever filled it in. Every application answers them with what you set here — work authorisation and sponsorship for the country the job is in, years of experience 7 unless you set another number, the certifications you hold, your degree from your CV. A wrong answer already on a page (from OptimHire or any other filler) is corrected, and Submit waits until they are right. Anything you change by hand on a page is left as you set it.
Separate with commas. For a job anywhere else, "Are you authorised to work…?" is answered No and "Will you need sponsorship?" Yes.
"Do you hold an AWS Certified … certification?" is answered Yes for each one listed (separate with commas).

Workday & sign-in

Every company's Workday site needs its own account before you can apply. During a run the automation signs in with these details — or creates the account with them if there is none yet — so Workday jobs are applied to instead of skipped. The password was made for you; keep it (or change it) to sign in to those sites yourself. Accounts you made before are signed in to with the passwords below; with emailed codes on, Workday's e-mail links (verify your account, reset your password) are opened for you.
At least 8 characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a symbol.
For accounts you made before (yourself or with another tool): each is tried once. At most 3 failed sign-ins per site a day, so an account is never locked; a site that still can't be signed in to is not tried again until you give its password below or press Retry.
